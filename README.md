## Overview

**VQube Technologies [VQT]** is an international industrial engineering consulting and technical manpower provider headquartered in Chennai, India. ## Core Solutions

### 1. Heavy Industrial Engineering
- **Cement Plants:** Pyroprocessing systems, rotary kiln burner aerodynamics, preheater cyclones, clinker coolers, heavy platework (process gas ducting, baghouses, transfer chutes), and structural FEA.
- **Mining & Minerals:** Crushing and screening plant layouts, beneficiation circuits, bulk transfer chutes engineered with Discrete Element Modeling (DEM), and slurry hydraulics.
- **Oil & Gas (Upstream & Downstream):** FEED packages, 3D piping routing in AVEVA E3D / AutoCAD Plant 3D, ASME Section VIII pressure vessels, and API 650/620 storage tanks.

### 2. Multi-Physics Numerical Simulation
- **Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD):** ANSYS Fluent modeling for burner combustion, cyclone pressure-drop reduction (≥ 15% fan power savings), and dust collection dispersion.
- **Finite Element Analysis (FEA):** ANSYS Mechanical static, dynamic vibration, buckling, and thermal fatigue analysis for heavy rotating equipment and structures.
- **Pipe Stress & Flexibility:** Rigorous CAESAR II analysis calculating thermal expansion, ASME B31.1/B31.3 code compliance, and rotating equipment nozzle loads (API 610/617).

### 3. Engineering Manpower Deputation
Pre-screened engineering professionals deployed to client plant sites globally or operating from our dedicated Chennai Offshore Global Delivery Center (GDC):

- Mechanical Equipment Engineers
- Civil & Structural Design Engineers (Tekla / STAAD)
- Process & Chemical Engineers
- Piping Layout & Stress Engineers (CAESAR II)
- Electrical & Power Systems Engineers
- Instrumentation & Control (I&C) Engineers
- Project Planning & Controls Specialists (Primavera P6)
- Procurement & Technical Expeditors
- QA/QC & Welding Inspection Engineers
- HSE (Health, Safety & Environment) Specialists
- Senior CAD Designers & GA Drafters
