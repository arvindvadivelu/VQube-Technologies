Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: JULY 2026
At VQube Technologies, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our website visitors, clients, and business partners. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or communicate with us. By using our website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.
01 / DATA ACQUISITION

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

Personal Information:

  • Your Name, Company Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Job Title.
  • Country and Business Office Address.
  • Information submitted through contact forms, consultation requests, or emails.
02 / PURPOSE & APPLICATION

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries, requests, and commercial consultation requirements.
  • Provide engineering design, technical advisory, numerical simulation (CFD/FEA), recruitment, and technical manpower support services.
  • Prepare engineering quotations, feasibility proposals, and tender submissions.
  • Improve our website performance, layout architecture, and user experience.
  • Communicate project updates, operational milestones, and corporate information.
  • Comply with statutory, legal, and regulatory obligations.
  • Protect our business operations from fraud and unauthorised access.
03 / WEB TELEMETRY

3. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar analytical technologies to improve functionality, analyze traffic patterns, and enhance browsing experience.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some website features and dynamic calculators may not function as intended.

04 / THIRD-PARTY GOVERNANCE

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information strictly with:

  • Service providers supporting our technical infrastructure and business operations.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, and legal counsel.
  • Government, statutory, or regulatory authorities when required by applicable law.
  • Clients or project partners where necessary to perform contracted engineering services.

All such disclosures are made only when appropriate and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

05 / INFRASTRUCTURE PROTECTION

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

While we strive to protect your information using commercial-grade security protocols, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely infallible.

06 / RETENTION TIMELINES

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, provide our engineering services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

08 / STATUTORY RIGHTS

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable jurisdictional data privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information held by us.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Request erasure or deletion of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent where processing was based on prior consent.
  • Request restriction or object to specific processing activities.
  • Lodge a complaint with the appropriate data protection regulatory authority.
09 / JURISDICTIONAL TRANSFERS

9. International Data Transfers

As VQube Technologies delivers engineering services and manpower deputation to clients worldwide, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your domicile. Appropriate statutory safeguards are applied where required.
