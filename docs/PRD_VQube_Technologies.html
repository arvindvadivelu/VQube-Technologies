1.1 Enterprise Background & Core Mandate

VQube Technologies [VQT] is an international engineering consultancy and technical workforce solutions provider operating out of its Global Delivery Center in Chennai, India. Rooted in the corporate philosophy of "Vector • Vision • Value", VQube delivers end-to-end multi-disciplinary engineering, high-fidelity numerical simulation, and skilled engineering manpower deputation to heavy process industries worldwide.

1.2 Market Problem Statement & Heavy Industry Friction

Heavy manufacturing and process engineering sectors (Cement, Mining & Minerals, Oil & Gas, Power Generation) face severe operational and project delivery challenges:

  • Late-Stage Fabrication & Erection Failures: Unidentified thermal expansion stresses, aerodynamic pressure drops, and structural resonance cause multimillion-dollar field modifications and commissioning delays.
  • Shortage of Multi-Disciplinary Engineering Talent: EPC contractors frequently face crippling shortages of certified piping stress engineers (CAESAR II), Tekla steel modelers, process CFD specialists, and licensed QA/QC inspectors.
  • Fragmented Engineering Hand-offs: Conceptual design, detailed 3D modeling, stress verification, and procurement packages are often executed by disconnected vendors, leading to clash issues and schedule slippage.
  • Digital Discovery & Procurement Inefficiency: Traditional engineering consultancies lack transparent, machine-readable specifications, preventing autonomous enterprise evaluation and rapid technical scoping.

1.3 The VQube Solution & Unique Value Proposition

VQube resolves these bottlenecks through an integrated, closed-loop delivery ecosystem combining:

  • Pre-Fabrication Multi-Physics Verification: Leveraging ANSYS Fluent (CFD), ANSYS Mechanical (FEA), and CAESAR II to eliminate duct turbulence, burner instability, and thermal stress cracks prior to IFC release.
  • Rapid Deployment Across 11 Engineering Disciplines: Pre-screened talent mobilized within 5 to 14 business days across onsite plant assignments or offshore dedicated teams.
  • Strict Stage-Gate Quality Control: Milestone-based engineering reviews at 30%, 60%, 90%, and 100% IFC issue ensure absolute compliance with ASME, ASTM, DIN, ISO, and API codes.
  • Ultra-High-Performance Digital Portal: Built on native semantic web standards with PWA offline caching, structured Schema.org graphs, and the emerging /llms.txt standard for next-generation automated sourcing.
The 6 Operating Pillars of VQube:
01. Trust & Integrity: Uncompromising technical transparency and milestone accountability.
02. Quality & Excellence: 100% code-compliant engineering verified by advanced numerical modeling.
03. Creativity & Innovation: Continuous optimization of plant aerodynamics, steel weight, and flow efficiency.
04. Partnership & Customer Focus: Flexible commercial engagement tailored to capital budget constraints.
05. Teamwork & Collaboration: Seamless integration with client owner teams, EPCs, and equipment OEMs.
06. Safety & Sustainability: Inherently safe designs, lower aerodynamic pressure losses, and reduced carbon intensity.

6 Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)

The engineering deliverables and digital infrastructure must satisfy strict operational, security, and quality constraints:

6.1 Performance & Latency Requirements (NFR-1)

  • Digital Platform: Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) ≤ 1.2s on mobile 4G; First Input Delay (FID) / INP ≤ 50ms; Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) = 0.00.
  • PWA Repeat Load Latency: Assets served from Service Worker cache must respond in ≤ 50ms (measured locally at ~25-35ms).
  • Simulation Report Turnaround: Standard CFD/FEA diagnostic package delivered within 7 to 21 business days from geometry freeze.

6.2 Reliability, Availability & Resilience (NFR-2)

  • Web Availability: 99.95% uptime hosted on global edge CDN with automated failover.
  • Offline Functionality: Precached core pages (index.html, Contact.html, services.md) must render fully even when network connectivity drops to zero.
  • Drawing Integrity: All CAD files and calculations backed up incrementally daily with offsite encrypted replication.

6.3 Information Security, IP & Confidentiality (NFR-3)

  • Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA): Executed prior to exchanging proprietary CAD files, plant diagrams, or operational data.
  • Intellectual Property (IP) Transfer: 100% of generated CAD drawings, simulation models, and calculation sheets transfer to the client upon final milestone settlement.
  • Data Governance: Full compliance with ISO 27001 data isolation practices; client drawings stored in dedicated project repositories with role-based access control (RBAC).

6.4 Accessibility & Cross-Platform Compatibility (NFR-4)

  • Accessibility: Adherence to WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines, including minimum 4.5:1 color contrast ratios, full keyboard navigability, and ARIA labels.
  • Viewport Adaptability: Flawless rendering from 320px mobile displays up to 4K ultra-wide engineering monitors.
  • Browser Matrix: Full parity across Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari (iOS/macOS), and standard embedded WebViews.

6.5 Code Quality, Standards & Maintainability (NFR-5)

  • Engineering Code Alignment: Mandatory compliance with ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code (BPVC), ASME B31.1/B31.3, API 650/620, ASTM, DIN, and ISO 9001.
  • Software Maintainability: Strict separation of concerns, zero inline styling in production templates, semantic markup, and zero external runtime npm dependencies for the client frontend.

10.1 Definition of Ready (DoR) & Definition of Done (DoD)

Definition of Ready (DoR) for Engineering:
  • Executed Client Contract & Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).
  • Approved Basis of Design (BOD) including operating temperatures, pressures, material specs, and seismic/wind parameters.
  • Approved 3D equipment boundary envelope or vendor general arrangements.
  • Designated client technical counterpart for Stage-Gate review sign-offs.
Definition of Done (DoD) for IFC Deliverables:
  • Zero outstanding clash detections in multi-discipline 3D model.
