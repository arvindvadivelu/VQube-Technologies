Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: JULY 2026 Global Data Governance Standard
At VQube Technologies, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our website visitors, clients, and business partners. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or communicate with us. By using our website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.
01 / DATA COLLECTION

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, Company Name, Email Address, Phone / WhatsApp Number, and Professional Job Title.
  • Corporate Information: Country of operation and official Business Office Address.
  • Project Submissions: Information submitted through our contact forms, consultation requests, technical specifications, or direct email inquiries.
02 / DATA UTILIZATION

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to project enquiries, technical requests, and commercial consultation requirements.
  • Delivering engineering design, technical advisory, numerical simulation (CFD/FEA), technical recruitment, and manpower deputation services.
  • Preparing engineering quotations, commercial proposals, and tender documentation.
  • Improving our website performance, user interface layout, and overall browsing experience.
  • Communicating project updates, operational milestones, and technical newsletters.
  • Complying with applicable legal, statutory, and regulatory obligations.
  • Protecting our business operations and digital assets from unauthorized access, fraud, or misuse.
03 / WEB TELEMETRY

3. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar analytical tracking technologies to enhance user navigation, measure traffic patterns, and optimize site functionality.

You can manage or disable cookie preferences through your individual browser settings; however, disabling certain cookies may impact the interactive behavior of consultation forms or dynamic interface elements.

04 / THIRD-PARTY GOVERNANCE

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We maintain a strict policy against selling, renting, or trading personal data. Disclosures are made strictly under the following controlled circumstances:

  • To vetted service providers supporting our IT infrastructure, web hosting, and operational telemetry.
  • To professional advisers, auditors, and legal representatives where necessary for governance.
  • To statutory, governmental, or regulatory authorities when mandated under applicable law.
  • To client representatives or verified project partners where essential for the execution of contracted engineering deliverables.

All third-party data handlers are bound by strict contractual confidentiality agreements.

05 / INFRASTRUCTURE SECURITY

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical, administrative, and physical security measures to safeguard personal information against unauthorized access, accidental alteration, unlawful disclosure, or destruction.

While we deploy high-grade encryption and access controls, no digital transmission across the internet or cloud infrastructure can be guaranteed 100% immune from security vulnerabilities.

06 / RETENTION SCHEDULE

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for the duration required to fulfill the operational purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, provide ongoing engineering consulting support, comply with statutory tax and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce contractual agreements.

07 / EXTERNAL PLATFORMS

7. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain outbound links to external third-party services, including LinkedIn, Google Maps, and industry associations. VQube Technologies is not responsible for the privacy practices, cookies, or content of these external domains.

08 / STATUTORY RIGHTS

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable data protection laws in your jurisdiction, you may hold the following statutory rights:

  • The right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to request rectification of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete data records.
  • The right to request erasure or deletion of personal data where retention is no longer justified.
  • The right to withdraw previously granted consent for specific communication channels.
  • The right to request restriction of or object to specific data processing workflows.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with an appropriate statutory data protection authority.
09 / INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

9. International Data Transfers

Because VQube Technologies supports heavy industrial engineering and manpower deployments for international clients, your data may be transferred to and processed on secure cloud nodes outside your home country. We ensure adequate contractual and statutory safeguards are maintained during cross-border transfers.

10 / POLICY UPDATES

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

VQube Technologies reserves the right to periodically update or revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changing regulatory requirements or operational practices. Revised editions will be published directly on this page with the updated effective date.
