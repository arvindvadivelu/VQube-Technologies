DATA GOVERNANCE VERIFIED

VQube Technologies adheres to strict global data governance. Personal and corporate technical specifications are never sold, rented, or compromised.

At VQube Technologies, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our website visitors, clients, and business partners. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or communicate with us.
Article 1

Introduction & Commitment

1.1 VQube Technologies respects the confidential and proprietary nature of all client data, technical project specifications, and personal contact details shared through our digital portals or direct correspondence.

Article 2

Information We Collect

2.1 We collect personal and corporate information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, Company Name, Email Address, Phone / WhatsApp Number, and Professional Job Title.
  • Corporate Information: Country of operation and official Business Office Address.
  • Project Submissions: Information submitted through our contact forms, consultation requests, technical specifications, or direct email inquiries.
Article 3

How We Use Your Information

3.1 We use the collected information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to project enquiries, technical requests, and commercial consultation requirements.
  • Delivering engineering design, technical advisory, numerical simulation (CFD/FEA), technical recruitment, and manpower deputation services.
  • Preparing engineering quotations, commercial proposals, and tender documentation.
  • Improving our website performance, user interface layout, and overall browsing experience.
  • Complying with applicable legal, statutory, and regulatory obligations.
Article 4

Information Sharing & Disclosure

4.1 We maintain a strict policy against selling, renting, or trading personal data. Disclosures are made strictly to vetted IT service providers, professional advisors, or statutory authorities when mandated under applicable law. All third-party handlers are bound by strict contractual confidentiality agreements.

Article 5

Data Security & Retention

5.1 We implement industry-standard technical, administrative, and physical security measures to safeguard personal and technical project information against unauthorized access, accidental alteration, or unlawful disclosure.

5.2 We retain personal information only for the duration required to fulfill the operational purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, provide ongoing engineering consulting support, comply with statutory tax and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce contractual agreements.
